FAQ

Questions, answered.

Need anything else? Open a ticket and ask before sending payment.

How do I buy a package?

Select a package, join the NitroHaven Discord, and open a ticket to confirm the price and availability. Then pay the agreed amount through Cash App and return to your ticket with payment confirmation.

What is your Cash App?

Our Cash App link is cash.app/$VXownsu. Confirm the total in your Discord ticket before sending money.

Where do I go after paying?

Join the NitroHaven Discord, open a ticket, and provide your package choice and payment confirmation. Payment is not automatically verified by this website.

Do you offer a discount?

Yes, the advertised offer is 10% off if you invite 10 people. Ask staff to verify your invites and confirm the discount before paying.

How quickly will I receive my order?

Delivery timing depends on package availability and staff response. Confirm the expected timeframe in your ticket before paying; this website does not promise instant delivery.

Do I need to share my Discord password or token?

No. Do not share your Discord password, account token, or two-factor authentication codes with anyone.

Can this website confirm my Cash App payment automatically?

No. The Cash App link opens Cash App, but the website cannot independently verify the payment. NitroHaven staff must confirm payment before processing your order.
